Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything you need to know before bringing your team onto Kiruko.

Which country is Kiruko built for?

Kiruko is built for anyone who needs to manage attendance, payroll and the work their staff carry out — it isn't tied to a single country or region. It's designed to fit the needs of SMEs in the HR world everywhere, and the app is modular enough to adapt to almost any business situation. Get in touch and we'll tell you where we're headed.

Do my employees need to install an app?

Employees use the Kiruko mobile app for clock-in, payslips, leave, tasks and more. For shared spaces, you can also run a kiosk on one device so a whole team clocks in and out from it — no personal phone required.

How does overtime get calculated?

A built-in statutory overtime engine applies the right rules — weekly thresholds, rest-day and public-holiday premiums — instead of a flat guess. Overtime is confirmed by the employer before it's paid, so payslips stay accurate and fair.

Can one person be both an employer and an employee?

Yes. Owners, admins and managers who are also part of the team can switch between the employer dashboard and the employee view from a single login — pick which side to enter when you sign in.

Which languages are supported?

The app ships in English, French, Malagasy and Hindi — so each person works in the language they're most comfortable with.

Is our data secure?

Access is role-based and configurable per company, sign-in uses trusted-device binding, and sensitive actions are written to an append-only audit trail. Worker reports route through a confidential channel by design. A user's sensitive personal data is never processed without their explicit authorisation.

How much does it cost?

You can start for free and invite your team. For pricing on larger teams and add-ons, book a demo and we'll put together a plan that fits.
