Delete Your Account

Last updated: 4 July 2026 Operator: Zilwa Eklere Ltd

This page explains how to delete your Kiruko account — operated by Zilwa Eklere Ltd — and what happens to your data when you do. It applies to the Kiruko mobile app and web dashboard.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Kiruko app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

If you own a company that still has other members, you will be asked to transfer ownership or remove your team first, so that no employee is left without an employer.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app — for example, you have uninstalled it or cannot sign in — email hello@kiruko.mu from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to verify that you own the account, and we aim to action verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login is disabled and your personal identifiers — name, email address, phone number and password — are permanently scrubbed.
  • Documents and receipt images you uploaded, and the underlying files, are deleted.
  • Your notifications and active sessions are purged.

What is retained, and for how long

Employment, payroll and audit records that your employer is legally required to keep — for example under Mauritian tax and labour law — are retained but de-identified: they no longer identify you personally and link only to an anonymised record. These are kept for the period required by applicable law and then removed. For these workplace records Kiruko acts as a processor on behalf of your employer, who is the data controller. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Contact

Questions about account or data deletion? Contact Zilwa Eklere Ltd at hello@kiruko.mu.
